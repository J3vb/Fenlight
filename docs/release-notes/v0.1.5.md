## Fenlight 0.1.5 (Early Alpha)

A gun fix. Launchers from 0.1.2 and later update themselves when you open them. Co-op needs everyone on 0.1.5.

### Fixes
- **Scopes and red dots work again.** Aiming the MR-28 (and the Corvid .338 and Ironwood .50) showed a solid black scope; now you see a zoomed view with a crosshair. The AR-17, VX-9 and BR-12 red dots are back.
- **Muzzle flash is back** on every gun. Released builds had been missing it.
