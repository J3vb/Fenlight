## Fenlight 0.1.1 (Early Alpha)

The first stable build. Install it with **Fenlight-Setup-0.1.1.exe** below; the launcher then keeps the game up to date on its own. Stable keeps its own characters, so you start fresh here. Characters from the test build stay in Test builds.

Everyone in a co-op game needs this version. Servers hosted from the launcher update themselves between matches.

### New
- **A new name and a new front end.** The game is now Fenlight, with a new main menu, a Loadout screen with five tabs and a launcher of its own (news, patch notes, Host a server).
- **Research.** Spend skill points from the main menu. Skills now really work: faster sprint, repair and revive, more points, less recoil, more reserve ammo, and rule changers like Last Stand, Undying, Execution Line, Bargain Hunter, Dead Center, Chain Supply and Overtime.
- **Field abilities.** Pick one under the Research tree: Shock Pulse, Med Burst, Adrenal Rush, Decoy Siren or the Watchdog turret.
- **Legendaries.** 14 Legendary items, each with its own trait, from Tidewater and Lantern Fall to Dead Man's Hand and Twin Pin. Some drop only as Legendaries.
- **Four new gear slots.** Headgear, gloves, backpacks and boots now drop, for nine slots in all. They don't show on your character yet.
- **Frag grenades.** Cook, throw, and clear a doorway.
- **Mantle** over waist- and chest-high ledges.
- **Six new power-ups:** Payday, Overkill, Stasis, Purge, Scrap Kit and the Supply Crate, with a timer ring on the HUD.
- **Blackout rounds.** The lights go out in Halcyon Labs and Glower packs hunt in the dark. Survive for a Resupply.
- **New zombie models** for the Shambler (two looks), Bloater and Crawler.
- **New HUD:** health and sprint panel, serum slots, weapon panel, round counter, compass and a clearer pick-up prompt.
- **Real reloads.** Magazine swaps by hand, a bolt catch on empty, the BR-12 loads shell by shell, and every move has its sound.
- **Wear gear mid-run** from the Tab Pack screen. Every gun and gear piece has an icon.
- **First person:** your sleeves follow your vest, your charm hangs on your gun and your implant sits on your forearm.
- **Halcyon Labs** has new machines, fuller rooms and small signs of life (a fan, PA crackle, loose papers).
- **Lantern Post** has four stall keepers who greet you and talk, and a concrete wall around town.

### Fixes
- The launcher no longer freezes ("Not responding") right after it opens on Windows. If you installed 0.1.0, run this setup once by hand.
- The game no longer hangs when you quit from the main menu.
- No more freezes mid-round, and the Settings dropdowns are filled in again.
- You can no longer walk through some map objects.
- Fixed sound spam, hold to repair, the red dot sight, ammo drops and the loot drop glow.
- The Pack key no longer clashes with co-op Pause and Settings.
- The main menu music is quieter and fades in.
