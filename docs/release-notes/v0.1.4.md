## Fenlight 0.1.4 (Early Alpha)

A launcher fix. Launchers from 0.1.2 and later update themselves when you open them. Co-op needs everyone on 0.1.4.

### Fixes
- **Launcher bottom bar stays in the window.** While installing, the bar no longer runs off the right edge and cuts off the install size and free space. This happened at every size, and most at 125% and 150% Windows display scaling. **Play** now keeps one width and fills up as the download goes, the percentage sits next to the download size, and **Cancel** is a small ✕ button.
- Longer launcher messages (like a failed download) now show on two lines under the game version, instead of being cut short.
